Central London

Sights

Museums, the river, and a couple of things just for the kids

🏛️

British MuseumBloomsbury · ~10 min walk

Host recommendation ⭐FreeFamily

The Rosetta Stone, the Parthenon sculptures and the Egyptian mummies, free and on the doorstep. It's close enough to visit in a few short trips rather than one long one.

🕐 Daily 10:00–17:00 (Fri until 20:30)☎️ +44 20 7323 8299
📍 Google Maps ☎️ Call
Tip: Book a free timed entry slot online to skip the queue. Go straight to Room 4 (Rosetta Stone) and Rooms 62–63 (mummies) before the crowds build.
🦕

Natural History MuseumSouth Kensington · further out

FreeFamily

Dinosaurs, the blue whale in Hintze Hall and the earthquake room. Allow a half or full day. The Piccadilly line from Leicester Square or Piccadilly Circus takes about 15 minutes.

🕐 Daily 10:00–17:50☎️ +44 20 7942 5000
📍 Google Maps ☎️ Call
🚀

Science MuseumSouth Kensington · next door to the NHM

FreeFamily

Right next to the Natural History Museum, so the two pair easily on one day. Space capsules, flight galleries and the hands-on Wonderlab. Entry is free; Wonderlab is paid and a hit with a 10-year-old.

🕐 Daily 10:00–18:00
📍 Google Maps
Tip: Do both museums on one day: the Natural History Museum in the morning, lunch, then the Science Museum in the afternoon. From South Kensington station, take the signposted pedestrian tunnel; it stays dry and brings you out at both museums. Book free timed slots in school holidays.
🎭

Covent GardenCovent Garden · ~10 min walk

FreeFamily

The piazza, the Apple Market stalls, shops, and street performers on the West Piazza. It's an easy walk from Soho and a good place to wander without a plan.

📍 Google Maps
🎨

Tate Modern + Millennium BridgeBankside

FreeFamily

Modern art in a converted power station. The huge Turbine Hall and the free viewing level at the top are the highlights. Cross the Millennium Bridge on foot to reach St Paul's.

🕐 Daily 10:00–18:00☎️ +44 20 7887 8888
📍 Tate Modern 📍 Millennium Bridge ☎️ Call
Tip: Do it as a walk: Tate Modern, then across the Millennium Bridge, then St Paul's Cathedral. Crossing the bridge takes about 10 minutes, with the dome straight ahead the whole way.
🎡

London Eye + Jubilee GardensSouth Bank

FamilyBook ahead

A slow 30-minute turn with views across Westminster. Jubilee Gardens, right beside it, has a playground and lawns where the family can take a break.

📍 London Eye 📍 Jubilee Gardens
Tip: Buy timed tickets online. They cost less than at the booth and the queue is shorter.
🌀

Museum of IllusionsFitzrovia · 15–17 Tottenham Court Rd

Family · 10 & 15

About an hour of optical tricks, tilted rooms and photo spots. It works for both a 10- and a 15-year-old and makes a good rainy-day option a few minutes' walk from the base.

📍 Google Maps
💎

Crystal Maze LIVE ExperienceWest End · 22–32 Shaftesbury Ave

Best for the teenBook ahead

The TV game show recreated for real: a team races through themed zones solving physical and mental challenges to win crystals. Best suited to the 15-year-old. Check the minimum age before bringing the 10-year-old.

📍 Google Maps

A bit further out

Full-day trips

Worth a whole day each: pick one, or both

🌿

Kew GardensRichmond · west London

FamilyBook ahead

The Royal Botanic Gardens: the Victorian Palm House, the Treetop Walkway 18m up, and a large Children's Garden. Take the District line to Kew Gardens station, about 45 minutes from the West End.

🕐 Opens daily 10:00 · closing time varies by season☎️ +44 20 8332 5655
📍 Google Maps ☎️ Call
⛵

Cutty SarkGreenwich · DLR or riverboat

Family

A 19th-century tea clipper you can walk underneath. Pair it with the Royal Observatory, where you can stand on the Prime Meridian, and the views from Greenwich Park. Take the DLR to Cutty Sark station or a Thames riverboat.

🕐 Daily 10:00–17:00
📍 Google Maps
Tip: Take the Uber Boat by Thames Clippers out from Embankment or London Eye pier, then come back on the DLR. Tapping on with contactless works on the boat too. The ride goes past the Tower of London and under Tower Bridge.

Food & Drink

Host favourites first, then Soho alternatives

Host favourites

🍳

Bill's SohoSoho · Brewer St

Host recommendation ⭐Family

The go-to breakfast: full English, pancakes and good coffee. It's relaxed, kid-friendly and a short walk from the base.

📍 Google Maps
🍷

Gordon's Wine BarEmbankment · 47 Villiers St

Host recommendation ⭐Best for adults

London's oldest wine bar: a candlelit, vaulted medieval cellar that feels unchanged in a century. It's mainly a grown-ups' evening; check the under-18 policy before bringing the kids.

🕐 Mon–Sat 11:00–23:00 · Sun 12:00–22:00☎️ +44 20 7930 1408
📍 Google Maps ☎️ Call
Tip: Gordon's doesn't take bookings. Arrive before 17:30 to get a table in the cellar.
🌆

Duck & WaffleCity · 40th floor, 110 Bishopsgate

Host recommendation ⭐FamilyBook ahead

A restaurant on the 40th floor, open 24 hours, with a view across the whole city. Order the signature dish, duck & waffle. It's worth doing at any hour.

🕐 Open 24 hours☎️ +44 20 3640 7310
📍 Google Maps ☎️ Call
Tip: Book ahead, especially for a sunset table. Breakfast is the easiest time to go with the kids, and the view is just as good.

Trisha'sSoho · 57 Greek St (basement)

Adults only

A members' basement dive bar that's been a Soho institution for decades. Visitors get in with ID. Expect cheap drinks, a jukebox and a guaranteed story. It is not for the kids.
