## Sights

### 🏛️ British Museum ⭐ *(Host recommendation)* · 👨‍👩‍👧‍👦 Family · Free
Bloomsbury, about 10 minutes' walk. The Rosetta Stone, the Parthenon sculptures and the Egyptian mummies, free and on the doorstep.
- 🕐 Daily 10:00–17:00 (Fri until 20:30)
- ☎️ +44 20 7323 8299
- 📍 [Google Maps](https://maps.google.com/?q=51.519413,-0.126957)

> **Tip:** Book a free timed entry slot online to skip the queue. Go straight to Room 4 (Rosetta Stone) and Rooms 62–63 (mummies) before the crowds build.

### 🦕 Natural History Museum · 👨‍👩‍👧‍👦 Family · Free
South Kensington, further out. Allow a half or full day. Dinosaurs, the blue whale in Hintze Hall and the earthquake room.
- 🕐 Daily 10:00–17:50
- ☎️ +44 20 7942 5000
- 📍 [Google Maps](https://maps.google.com/?q=51.496715,-0.176367)

### 🚀 Science Museum · 👨‍👩‍👧‍👦 Family · Free
South Kensington, right next to the Natural History Museum, so the two pair easily on one day. Space capsules, flight galleries and the hands-on Wonderlab (paid).
- 🕐 Daily 10:00–18:00
- 📍 [Google Maps](https://maps.google.com/?q=51.497825,-0.174466)

> **Tip:** Do both museums on one day: the Natural History Museum in the morning, lunch, then the Science Museum in the afternoon. From South Kensington station, take the signposted pedestrian tunnel; it stays dry and brings you out at both museums.

### 🎭 Covent Garden · 👨‍👩‍👧‍👦 Family · Free
The piazza, the Apple Market stalls, shops, and street performers on the West Piazza. It's an easy walk from Soho.
- 📍 [Google Maps](https://maps.google.com/?q=51.511910,-0.122762)

### 🎨 Tate Modern + Millennium Bridge · 👨‍👩‍👧‍👦 Family · Free
Bankside. Modern art in a converted power station, with the Turbine Hall and the free viewing level at the top as highlights.
- 🕐 Daily 10:00–18:00
- ☎️ +44 20 7887 8888
- 📍 [Google Maps — Tate Modern](https://maps.google.com/?q=51.507595,-0.099356)
- 📍 [Google Maps — Millennium Bridge](https://maps.google.com/?q=51.509540,-0.098440)

> **Tip:** Do it as a walk: Tate Modern, then across the Millennium Bridge, then [St Paul's Cathedral](https://maps.google.com/?q=51.513845,-0.098351). Crossing takes about 10 minutes.

### 🎡 London Eye + Jubilee Gardens · 👨‍👩‍👧‍👦 Family
South Bank. A 30-minute turn with views across Westminster. Jubilee Gardens, right beside it, has a playground and lawns for a break.
- 📍 [Google Maps — London Eye](https://maps.google.com/?q=51.503324,-0.119543)
- 📍 [Google Maps — Jubilee Gardens](https://maps.google.com/?q=51.504200,-0.118900)

> **Tip:** Buy timed tickets online. They cost less than at the booth and the queue is shorter.

### 🌀 Museum of Illusions · 👨‍👩‍👧‍👦 Family (10 & 15)
Fitzrovia, 15–17 Tottenham Court Road. About an hour of optical tricks and photo spots. It works for both a 10- and a 15-year-old.
- 📍 [Google Maps](https://maps.google.com/?q=51.516800,-0.131000)

### 💎 Crystal Maze LIVE Experience · 🧑 Best for the teen
22–32 Shaftesbury Avenue. The TV game show recreated for real, with team challenges across themed zones. Best suited to the 15-year-old. Check the minimum age when booking.
- 📍 [Google Maps](https://maps.google.com/?q=51.510500,-0.133000)

## Full-day trips (a bit further out)

### 🌿 Kew Gardens · 👨‍👩‍👧‍👦 Family
Richmond, west London. The Palm House, the Treetop Walkway and the Children's Garden. Take the District line to Kew Gardens, about 45 minutes.
- 🕐 Opens daily 10:00 (closing time varies by season)
- ☎️ +44 20 8332 5655
- 📍 [Google Maps](https://maps.google.com/?q=51.478700,-0.295600)

### ⛵ Cutty Sark · 👨‍👩‍👧‍👦 Family
Greenwich, by DLR or riverboat. A 19th-century tea clipper you can walk underneath. Pair it with the Royal Observatory and Greenwich Park.
- 🕐 Daily 10:00–17:00
- 📍 [Google Maps](https://maps.google.com/?q=51.482700,-0.009600)

> **Tip:** Take the Uber Boat by Thames Clippers out from Embankment or London Eye pier, and come back on the DLR.

## Food & Drink

### 🍳 Bill's Soho ⭐ *(Host recommendation)* · 👨‍👩‍👧‍👦 Family
The go-to breakfast on Brewer Street. It's relaxed, kid-friendly and a short walk away.
- 📍 [Google Maps](https://maps.google.com/?q=51.511400,-0.136100)

### 🍷 Gordon's Wine Bar ⭐ *(Host recommendation)* · 🔞 Best for adults
A candlelit, vaulted medieval cellar at 47 Villiers Street, Embankment. London's oldest wine bar.
- 🕐 Mon–Sat 11:00–23:00 · Sun 12:00–22:00
- ☎️ +44 20 7930 1408
- 📍 [Google Maps](https://maps.google.com/?q=51.507800,-0.123200)

> **Tip:** Gordon's doesn't take bookings. Arrive before 17:30 to get a table in the cellar.

### 🌆 Duck & Waffle ⭐ *(Host recommendation)* · 👨‍👩‍👧‍👦 Family
On the 40th floor at 110 Bishopsgate, open 24 hours, with a view across the whole city.
- 🕐 Open 24 hours
- ☎️ +44 20 3640 7310
- 📍 [Google Maps](https://maps.google.com/?q=51.516200,-0.080900)

> **Tip:** Book ahead, especially for sunset. Breakfast is the easiest time to go with the kids.

### 🥑 Soho brunch alternatives · 👨‍👩‍👧‍👦 Family
- **Scarlett Green** (4 Noel St): Australian-style brunch. 📍 [Google Maps](https://maps.google.com/?q=51.515300,-0.136300)
- **Nessa Soho** (86 Brewer St): modern British bistro brunch. 📍 [Google Maps](https://maps.google.com/?q=51.511000,-0.135800)
- **Milk Beach Soho** (14 Greek St): bright all-day café. 📍 [Google Maps](https://maps.google.com/?q=51.514400,-0.131100)

### 🔥 Soho evening gems
- **Impala** (13–14 Dean St): North African grill, cooking over fire. 📍 [Google Maps](https://maps.google.com/?q=51.515500,-0.133000)
- **The Soho Social** (9 Berwick St): a relaxed spot for evening drinks and food. 📍 [Google Maps](https://maps.google.com/?q=51.513900,-0.134800)

## Adults only

### 🍸 Trisha's · 🔞 Adults only
A members' basement dive bar at 57 Greek Street. Visitors need ID. It is not for the kids.
- 📍 [Google Maps](https://maps.google.com/?q=51.513600,-0.131400)

> **Tip:** Bring photo ID and ring the buzzer. Go late, while another adult covers the kids.
